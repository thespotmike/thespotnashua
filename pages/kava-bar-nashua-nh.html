The Only Kava Bar in Nashua, NH

If you've been searching for a kava bar near Nashua, you've found it. The Spot opened at 217 Main Street in downtown Nashua to give the city something it didn't have: a real social lounge where you can relax, connect, and enjoy great drinks without any alcohol involved.

Kava is a naturally calming drink made from the root of the Piper methysticum plant, native to the South Pacific. It's been used for centuries in Polynesian culture as a social and ceremonial drink. At The Spot, we serve it the traditional way -- in a shell -- and in modern blended kava drinks that are smooth, earthy, and genuinely relaxing. No hangover. Not addictive. Just good vibes.

What to Expect on Your First Visit

Walk in and you'll find a cozy, lounge-style space -- think comfortable seating, warm lighting, and a bar that actually looks like a bar. We get a lot of first-timers who are curious about kava but not sure what they're walking into. Our staff will walk you through everything: what kava tastes like, what to expect, how much to start with, and what we'd recommend based on what you're in the mood for.

Beyond kava, we have the full drinks menu -- coffee drinks, mocktails, and non-alcoholic options for anyone who wants something familiar. There's no cover charge. No membership. Walk-ins are welcome anytime we're open.

A Kava Bar With a Live Music Venue Attached

The Spot isn't just a kava bar -- it's the closest thing Nashua has to a full-service music and entertainment venue that doesn't require a drink ticket to get in. We host live music every Friday and Saturday, plus open mic nights, DJ sets, trivia, karaoke, and community events throughout the week. No cover charge on most nights.

If you're a musician looking to book a show in Nashua, you can submit a booking request here. We welcome solo artists, acoustic acts, bands of all sizes, and DJs.

Alcohol-Free and All-Ages

The Spot is fully alcohol-free -- we don't serve beer, wine, or spirits, and we're not licensed as a traditional bar. That's intentional. Our founder Mike McDowell has been sober for over 18 years, and The Spot was built on the belief that you shouldn't need alcohol to have a great night out. We're all-ages, which means you can bring your 19-year-old who can't get into a bar, your 45-year-old coworker who doesn't drink, and your friend who's been sober for a year. Everyone is welcome here.

We're also the kind of place where people bring their laptops in the afternoon, hang out for hours without feeling rushed, and come back the next week. If you've never had a real "third place" in Nashua -- somewhere that's not home and not work -- this might be it.
