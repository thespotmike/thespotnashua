What is The Spot? The Spot is an alcohol-free kava bar and live music venue at 217 Main Street in downtown Nashua, NH. We serve premium kava, craft coffee, mocktails, and live entertainment. Open Monday through Thursday 3PM-11PM, Friday and Saturday 3PM-1AM.

Does The Spot serve alcohol? No. The Spot is 100% alcohol-free. Every drink on our menu is non-alcoholic, making us welcoming to everyone including those in recovery.

Where is The Spot located? 217 Main Street, Nashua, NH 03060. Call us at (603) 718-4732.

Nashua's Alcohol-Free Kava Bar & Live Music Venue

The Spot is downtown Nashua's kava bar and live music venue at 217 Main Street. We serve premium kava, craft coffee from Anchorhead Coffee, creative mocktails, and small bites, all in an alcohol-free, all-ages social lounge.

Open Monday through Thursday 3–11PM and Friday–Saturday 3PM–1AM, The Spot hosts live music, open mic nights, DJ sets, trivia, karaoke, comedy, and community events every week. No cover charge. No alcohol. Just good vibes.
